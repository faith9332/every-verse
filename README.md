# Every Verse

A Bible reading tracker and journal for your phone. Tick off every chapter of the Bible (66 books, 1,189 chapters, 31,102 verses), log the dates you read, keep notes on each chapter, and follow your progress.

**Open the app:** https://faith9332.github.io/every-verse/

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Every Verse from its icon. After the first visit it works without internet.

## Your data

Your reading log and notes are stored only on your own phone. Nothing is sent anywhere and no account is needed. Use **Settings → Export backup** now and then, and **Import a backup** to restore on a new phone.
